## Textbook

I co-authored [**Deep and Shallow: Machine Learning in Music and Audio**](https://www.routledge.com/Deep-and-Shallow-Machine-Learning-in-Music-and-Audio/Dubnov-Greer/p/book/9781032133911) with Shlomo Dubnov. The book introduces techniques in signal processing, machine learning fundamentals, and representation learning, shown with applications to music and audio.
